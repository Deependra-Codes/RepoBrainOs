## Purpose

Research documents should answer one of these:

- What is the market gap?
- What is the strongest product framing?
- What do recent papers imply for architecture choices?
- What retrieval or grounding policy is realistic for production?
- What execution discipline keeps AI-assisted coding rigorous?
- What failure modes or risks should shape the design?

## Rule

Research can be bold.

Architecture docs must translate research into bounded, honest claims.
